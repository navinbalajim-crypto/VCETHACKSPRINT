Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in HackSprint '26 — eligibility criteria, the sole Agentic AI track, compute sandboxes, overnight accommodations, evaluation parameters, and the ₹30,000 cash pool.

1. Eligibility & General Verification

HackSprint '26 is open to all enrolled undergraduate (B.E., B.Tech, B.Sc, BCA) and postgraduate (M.E., M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc) students from AICTE-approved and UGC-recognized engineering colleges and universities across India. All participants must present a valid College Identity Card during on-site biometric check-in on September 30, 2026.

Yes! We actively encourage inter-college squads as well as cross-department teams (combining AI/DS, IT, CSE, ECE, Mechanical, etc.). Cross-disciplinary synergy is especially powerful in Agentic AI where hardware integration, system security, and UX interact intimately.

Individual registrations are not permitted. HackSprint '26 requires teams of strictly 4 members (fixed squad size). Agentic AI development involves multi-agent orchestration, tool engineering, frontend integration, and sandbox testing — making team collaboration essential within the 24-hour sprint.

2. Team Formation & Registration Fee

The registration fee is fixed at ₹500 per participant (₹2,000 for the full 4-member squad)

Payment is processed via direct UPI to the official institutional account (Official UPI QR code on the Google Registration Form). During the 4-step registration on our portal, you will scan the dynamic QR code and enter the 12-digit UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) number. Our finance desk verifies and issues an instant squad dispatch confirmation.

Yes, roster adjustments are permitted until September 25, 2026 (5:00 PM IST). The registered Team Leader must email hacksprint26@gmail.com with the original squad registration ID, student ID proof of both departing and replacement members, and endorsement from their college.

3. Agentic AI Track & Technical Sandboxes

Traditional AI generates static text and code; Agentic AI executes goal-driven autonomous actions. Agentic AI represents the bleeding edge of software engineering — systems capable of self-reflection, planning, tool-use, multi-agent negotiation, and environment actuation. HackSprint '26 focuses exclusively on this frontier across 6 specialized vectors (Multi-Agent Swarms, Autonomous Dev, Computer-Use, Edge/Embodied AI, Autonomous Defense, and Decision Intelligence).

You are encouraged to utilize established agentic frameworks such as CrewAI, AutoGen 0.4+, LangGraph, LlamaIndex Workflows, Semantic Kernel, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). For local model execution, Ollama, vLLM, and llama.cpp are fully permitted. All code written during the 24 hours must be committed to your team's designated repository.

Both are permitted! VCET IT provides on-premise local server clusters pre-loaded with quantized weights for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen-2.5-Coder-7B, and DeepSeek-R1-Distill accessible via an OpenAI-compatible endpoint over local LAN. Teams are also completely free to consume their own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, Groq, or Together AI).

Every team member must bring their own laptop, charger, power extension strip (spike buster), and necessary peripheral cables. For teams competing in the Edge & Embodied Agentic AI vector, you are encouraged to bring microcontrollers (Raspberry Pi 4/5, ESP32, Jetson Nano, camera modules, sensors, or robotic arms).

4. Campus Logistics, Food & Accommodations

Yes, HackSprint '26 is a continuous 24-hour sprint running from 10:00 AM on September 30 to 10:00 AM on October 1. Dedicated overnight air-conditioned resting bays with mattresses and resting zones are provided separately for male and female participants within the VCET IT complex. The campus is monitored 24/7 by trained campus security and faculty marshals.

Full dining service is provided:

  • Day 1 Lunch (01:00 PM): Wholesome South Indian & Continental Buffet
  • Evening High Tea (05:30 PM): Madurai special savories, filter coffee & cookies
  • Day 1 Dinner (09:00 PM): Premium Veg / Non-Veg Dinner
  • Midnight Power Pitstop (01:30 AM): Fresh filter coffee, Red Bull energy drinks, pastries
  • Day 2 Breakfast (07:30 AM): Hot Idli, Vada, Pongal, Poori & tea

VCET campus features a dual-redundant 1 Gbps dedicated leased-line fiber network with Enterprise Aruba WiFi 6 access points deployed across all coding labs. In addition, every hacker workstation has access to Gigabit RJ45 Ethernet drops for ultra-low latency model fine-tuning and agent streaming.

5. Judging Rubric & ₹30,000 Prize Pool

Projects are assessed by an elite jury of principal AI architects and faculty using a transparent 100-point rubric:

  • Autonomous Decision-Making & Planning (30%): Depth of reasoning, multi-step subtask breakdown, reflection loops, and avoidance of hardcoded logic.
  • Tool Execution & MCP Integration (25%): Seamless schema calling, real API execution, external sandbox safety, and protocol compliance.
  • Self-Correction & Resilience (20%): How gracefully the agent handles synthetic API timeouts, invalid tool inputs, and edge failures.
  • Production Feasibility & Impact (15%): Real-world market viability, latency profiles, token-efficiency, and user experience.
  • Pitch & Live Demonstration (10%): Clarity of the 7-minute technical pitch, architecture diagrams, and live system actuation.

The grand prize purse of ₹30,000 is awarded directly to the winning teams at the valedictory ceremony on October 1, 2026:

  • Grand Champion (First Place): ₹15,000 cash prize + VCET IT Winner Trophy + Premium Cloud Credits
  • First Runner-Up (Second Place): ₹10,000 cash prize + Silver Trophy + Mentorship Credits
  • Second Runner-Up (Third Place): ₹5,000 cash prize + Bronze Trophy + Toolchain Perks
  • All Finalists: Certificate of Technical Excellence & fast-track recruitment opportunities with sponsor startups.

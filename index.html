# MISSION & ARCHITECTURE

A platform built for intelligent problem-solving.

HackSprint '26 is the flagship national hackathon curated by the Department of Information Technology at Velammal College of Engineering & Technology, Madurai.

We bring together 500+ handpicked software engineers, designers, AI researchers, and hardware builders. Over 24 intensive hours, teams collaborate under mentorship from prominent industry architects to build production-grade prototypes addressing critical societal challenges.

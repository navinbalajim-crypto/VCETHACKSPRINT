# SOLE OFFICIAL THEME

Theme: Agentic AI

Moving beyond conversational chatbots. HackSprint '26 challenges 4-member builder squads to architect autonomous software agents capable of proactive perception, multi-stage planning, sandboxed tool usage, and self-correcting execution.

NATIONAL 24-HOUR AUTONOMOUS CODE SPRINT

Pillars of Agentic AI

PILLAR 01

Multimodal Grounding

Perceive unstructured environments, terminal streams, GUI windows, and code ASTs with grounded spatial and semantic context.

PILLAR 02

Cognitive Planning

Formulate dynamic ReAct reasoning chains, hierarchical goal trees, and peer-to-peer delegation graphs without hardcoded scripts.

PILLAR 03

Sandboxed Tool Use

Safely invoke local and cloud microservices, Docker testbeds, system terminals, and vector databases via standardized tool interfaces.

PILLAR 04

Reflection & Self-Healing

Inspect tool failure traces, reflect on sub-goal drift, synthesize regression tests, and autonomously correct errors before final output.

# OFFICIAL TIMELINE

24-Hour Event Schedule

Continuous 24-hour overnight schedule running from 9:00 AM on Day 1 (Sept 30) to 9:30 AM on Day 2 (Oct 1, 2026), ending with the ₹25,000 Grand Valedictory Function.

📅 DAY 1 · 30.09.2026 (INAUGURATION & CORE SPRINT)
09:00 – 09:30 AM · 30.09.2026

Participant Registration & Kit Distribution

Check-in at VCET Auditorium foyer. Verification of student IDs, workstation setup, Wi-Fi credentials, and official delegate kits.

09:30 – 10:30 AM · 30.09.2026

Theme, Problem Statements, Rules & Guidelines Briefing

Official commencement and briefing on Agentic AI challenge tracks, judging rubrics, rules, and API sandbox guidelines.

10:30 – 11:30 AM · 30.09.2026

Ideation Phase of Selected Problem Statements

Squads brainstorm and lock in problem statements. Formulation of agent architecture graphs, tool orchestration DAGs, and preliminary system design.

11:30 AM – 12:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Tea & Refreshments

Morning hot tea, artisanal filter coffee, and light refreshments served at the venue to recharge teams.

12:00 PM – 01:30 PM · 30.09.2026

Development Phase – I

Development environments configured, foundation model APIs linked, baseline agent scaffolding assembled, and primary loops launched.

01:30 – 02:15 PM · 30.09.2026

Lunch

Grand multi-course networking lunch buffet provided for all participants, mentors, and faculty at the VCET dining facility.

02:15 – 03:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Development Phase – II

Deep implementation sprint: core tool integration, agent memory setup, function-calling execution loops, and autonomous workflow design.

03:00 – 04:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Review – I (Idea/Architecture Review)

Senior technical mentors and academic evaluators review system architecture diagrams, safety boundaries, and API integrations.

04:00 – 05:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Development Phase – III

Intensive development sprint incorporating Review I mentor guidance: multi-agent consensus, retrieval pipelines, and core integration.

05:00 – 05:30 PM · 30.09.2026

Tea & Refreshments

Evening hot tea, artisanal filter coffee, and savory energy snacks served at the engineering floor to fuel team velocity.

05:30 – 08:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Development Phase – IV

Advanced multi-agent implementation: tool chaining, validation safeguards, context routing, and subsystem integration.

08:00 – 08:30 PM · 30.09.2026

Dinner

Nutritious campus dinner buffet served at campus dining hall; squads align overnight sprint tasks and team roles.

08:30 – 10:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Development Phase – V

Deep feature buildout, backend API orchestration, agent tool execution testing, and prompt chain refinement.

10:00 – 11:00 PM · 30.09.2026

Review – II (Development Progress Review)

Deep technical review by domain specialists assessing live execution progress, error resilience, and agent autonomy.

11:00 PM – 01:00 AM · 30.09.2026 – 01.10.2026

Development Phase – VI

Late-night intensive sprint: complex sub-goal execution, autonomous self-healing loops, and agent optimization.

🏆 DAY 2 · 01.10.2026 (OVERNIGHT SPRINT, REVIEWS & VALEDICTORY)
01:00 – 01:30 AM · 01.10.2026

Midnight Refreshments

Artisanal filter coffee, tea, and high-energy snacks served at workstations to fuel late-night development.

01:30 – 03:00 AM · 01.10.2026

Development Phase – VII

Deep overnight sprint: polishing user interfaces, telemetry dashboards, end-to-end autonomous agent execution, and benchmark testing.

03:00 – 04:00 AM · 01.10.2026

Review – III (Prototype & Integration Review)

Third evaluation round. Jury mentors evaluate working prototype execution, multi-agent coordination stability, and deliverable readiness.

04:00 – 05:00 AM · 01.10.2026

Development Phase – VIII

Post-review refinements: resolving edge cases identified in Review III, hardening error recovery, and final pipeline stabilization.

05:00 – 05:30 AM · 01.10.2026

Tea / Refreshments

Early dawn hot tea, coffee, and light refreshments as squads power into the final hours of the hackathon sprint.

05:30 – 07:30 AM · 01.10.2026

Final Development, Integration & Testing

Cloud deployment, container verification, automated regression tests, pitch deck preparation, and final functional checks.

07:30 – 08:45 AM · 01.10.2026

Final Review (Project Demo & Jury Evaluation)

Live interactive autonomous agent demonstrations, technical defense, and final scoring before the grand jury panels.

08:45 – 09:30 AM · 01.10.2026

Breakfast

Hot South Indian breakfast buffet served at the VCET dining hall for all participants, mentors, and faculty evaluators.

09:30 AM · 01.10.2026

Results / Valedictory Function

Grand valedictory ceremony, announcement of winners, presentation of the ₹25,000 total prize pool, and certificate distribution.

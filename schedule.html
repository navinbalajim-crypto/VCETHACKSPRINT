24-Hour Run-of-Show

From the initial opening ceremony to the 10:00 AM terminal shutdown and the 25K Grand Valedictory, here is your minute-by-minute blueprint for September 30 – October 1, 2026.

09:00 AM – 09:30 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Registration & Squad Check-in

Physical check-in at VCET Auditorium foyer. Verification of college ID cards, biometric verification, allocation of designated hardware workstations, and distribution of official HackSprint '26 kits.

09:30 AM – 10:00 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Inauguration Ceremony

Grand formal inauguration presided over by Patron Dr. P. Alli (Principal, VCET), Convener Dr. R. Kavitha (HoD/IT), IEEE Student Branch dignitaries, and industry leaders from sponsor BSE-TEC.

10:00 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026) · CLOCK STARTS

Hackathon Commences (24-Hour Non-Stop Sprint)

The 24-hour non-stop countdown begins! GitHub repositories initialized, compute clusters connected, agent architecture graphs activated, and multi-agent coordination loops deployed.

01:00 PM – 02:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Lunch Break

Grand multi-course networking lunch buffet served at the VCET Dining Hall. Recharge, connect with fellow builders, and review initial vector diagrams.

04:00 PM – 04:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Refreshment & High Tea

Evening hot coffee, tea, and savory energy snacks provided at the engineering arena. Quick technical standup for all squads.

05:00 PM – 07:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Mentoring Round 1 (Idea & Architectural Review)

Senior faculty mentors, IEEE technical evaluators, and BSE-TEC system architects visit each workstation to assess architectural soundness, tool invocation safety, and problem scope alignment.

08:30 PM – 09:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Dinner Break

Wholesome dinner served at campus dining. Squads synchronize task pipelines and distribute overnight coding modules.

11:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Midnight Energy Boost & Coffee Break

Fresh brewed artisanal filter coffee, energizing treats, and midnight music lounge to power squads through the deep overnight coding hours.

01:00 AM – 03:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Mentoring Round 2 (Code Review & Progress Assessment)

Overnight code-level sanity check. Technical mentors inject edge-case scenarios to verify error-handling, tool recovery, and real-time execution resilience.

06:30 AM – 07:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Breakfast Break

Hot South Indian breakfast served. Teams run final build scripts, polish user interfaces, and finalize live demonstration pipelines.

08:30 AM – 09:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Final Presentation & Evaluation Round

Formal jury review before distinguished panels of industry and academic evaluators. Each squad demonstrates end-to-end agentic workflows and quantitative results.

10:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026) · CLOCK STOPS

Hackathon Concludes (Terminal Shutdown)

Official 24-hour sprint concludes! All repository commits locked; jury tallies final scores across innovation, technical execution, and autonomy.

11:00 AM – 12:30 PM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Valedictory Ceremony & Prize Distribution

Grand presentation of the 25K Total Prize Pool, distribution of IEEE-backed certificates, and closing felicitations.

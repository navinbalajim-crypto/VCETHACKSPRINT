24-Hour Run-of-Show

Continuous 24-hour overnight schedule running from 9:00 AM on Day 1 (Sept 30) to 10:00 AM on Day 2 (Oct 1, 2026), ending with the ₹25,000 Grand Valedictory Function.

09:00 AM – 10:00 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Participation Registration & Kit Distribution

Physical check-in at the VCET Auditorium foyer. Verification of college ID cards, workstation setup, network credential allocation, and distribution of official HackSprint '26 delegate kits.

10:00 AM – 10:30 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Theme, Problem Statements & Guidelines

Official inauguration and challenge briefing. Institutional leaders, faculty conveners, and industry partners unveil the Agentic AI problem statements, judging rubrics, and sandbox guidelines.

10:30 AM – 01:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Ideation Phase of Selected Problem Statements

Squads brainstorm and lock in problem statements. Formulation of agent architecture graphs, tool orchestration DAGs, multi-agent communication protocols, and preliminary system design.

01:00 PM – 02:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Lunch

Grand multi-course networking lunch buffet served at the VCET Dining Hall. Builders recharge, connect with peers, and discuss architectural solutions.

02:00 PM – 04:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – I

Hands-on sprint kicks off! Development environments configured, model APIs linked, baseline scaffolding erected, and primary autonomous agent loops established.

04:00 PM – 05:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Review – I (Idea / Architecture)

First formal evaluation round. Senior faculty mentors and industry experts visit team stations to review architectural soundness, tool invocation boundaries, and technical feasibility.

05:00 PM – 05:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Tea Refreshment

Evening hot tea, artisanal filter coffee, and savory energy snacks provided at the engineering floor to fuel team velocity.

05:30 PM – 09:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – II

Intensive development sprint incorporating Review I mentor guidance. Multi-agent consensus, retrieval-augmented memory, function calling pipelines, and core integration.

09:00 PM – 10:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Dinner

Nutritious campus dinner buffet served at VCET dining. Teams synchronize overnight coding roles and distribute remaining sprint tasks.

10:00 PM – 11:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Review – II (Progress / Technical)

Second evaluation round. Domain specialists conduct deep code inspections, evaluate error recovery and autonomous self-correction, and provide actionable overnight recommendations.

11:00 PM – 01:00 AM · OVERNIGHT (30.09 – 01.10.2026)

Development Phase – III

Late-night intensive sprint. Hardening agent reasoning capabilities, debugging edge cases, optimizing latency, and stabilizing multi-agent coordination under load.

01:00 AM – 01:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Midnight Refreshment

Midnight fresh filter coffee, hot tea, and energy treats served to fuel developers through the overnight coding stretch.

01:30 AM – 05:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Development Phase – IV

Overnight development sprint. Polishing user interfaces, telemetry dashboards, end-to-end user workflows, and comprehensive unit tests.

05:00 AM – 05:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Refreshment

Early dawn hot tea, coffee, and light refreshments as squads power into the final hours of the hackathon sprint.

05:30 AM – 07:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Final Deployment, Integration Testing & Documentation

Cloud deployment, container verification, automated regression tests, pitch deck preparation, and GitHub README documentation finalized.

07:30 AM – 08:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Breakfast

Hot South Indian breakfast buffet served at the VCET dining hall for all participants and evaluators before final submissions.

08:30 AM – 09:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026) · CODE FREEZE

Final Submission

Strict code freeze and repository lock! All GitHub repositories, demo screencasts, and slide decks submitted via the official portal.

09:00 AM – 10:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Project Demo & Final Review

Live interactive demonstrations and technical defenses before the grand jury panels. Scored across autonomy, novelty, architecture, and execution.

10:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026) · GRAND FINALE

Valedictory Function

Grand valedictory ceremony and prize distribution. Announcement of winners, presentation of the ₹25,000 cash prizes, IEEE certificates, and closing felicitations.

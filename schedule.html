09:00 AM – 09:30 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Participant Registration & Kit Distribution

Physical check-in at the VCET Auditorium foyer. Verification of college ID cards, workstation setup, network credential allocation, and distribution of official HackSprint '26 delegate kits.

09:30 AM – 10:30 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Theme, Problem Statements, Rules & Guidelines Briefing

Official inauguration and challenge briefing. Institutional leaders, faculty conveners, and industry partners unveil the Agentic AI problem statements, judging rubrics, and sandbox guidelines.

10:30 AM – 11:30 AM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Ideation Phase of Selected Problem Statements

Squads brainstorm and lock in problem statements. Formulation of agent architecture graphs, tool orchestration DAGs, multi-agent communication protocols, and preliminary system design.

11:30 AM – 12:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Tea & Refreshments

Morning hot tea, artisanal filter coffee, and light refreshments served at the venue to fuel builders for the first development sprint.

12:00 PM – 01:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – I

Hands-on sprint kicks off! Development environments configured, model APIs linked, baseline scaffolding erected, and primary autonomous agent loops established.

01:30 PM – 02:15 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Lunch

Grand multi-course networking lunch buffet served at the VCET Dining Hall. Builders recharge, connect with peers, and discuss architectural solutions.

02:15 PM – 03:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – II

Core tool integration, agent memory setup, function-calling execution loops, and autonomous workflow design.

03:00 PM – 04:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Review – I (Idea/Architecture Review)

First formal evaluation round. Senior faculty mentors and industry experts visit team stations to review architectural soundness, tool invocation boundaries, and technical feasibility.

04:00 PM – 05:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – III

Intensive development sprint incorporating Review I mentor guidance: multi-agent consensus, retrieval pipelines, and core integration.

05:00 PM – 05:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Tea & Refreshments

Evening hot tea, artisanal filter coffee, and savory energy snacks provided at the engineering floor to fuel team velocity.

05:30 PM – 08:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – IV

Advanced multi-agent implementation: tool chaining, validation safeguards, context routing, and subsystem integration.

08:00 PM – 08:30 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Dinner

Nutritious campus dinner buffet served at VCET dining hall. Teams synchronize overnight coding roles and distribute remaining sprint tasks.

08:30 PM – 10:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Development Phase – V

Deep feature buildout, backend API orchestration, agent tool execution testing, and prompt chain refinement.

10:00 PM – 11:00 PM · DAY 1 (30.09.2026)

Review – II (Development Progress Review)

Second evaluation round. Domain specialists conduct deep code inspections, evaluate error recovery and autonomous self-correction, and provide actionable overnight recommendations.

11:00 PM – 01:00 AM · OVERNIGHT (30.09 – 01.10.2026)

Development Phase – VI

Late-night intensive sprint: hardening agent reasoning capabilities, debugging edge cases, optimizing latency, and stabilizing multi-agent coordination under load.

01:00 AM – 01:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Midnight Refreshments

Midnight fresh filter coffee, hot tea, and energy treats served to fuel developers through the overnight coding stretch.

01:30 AM – 03:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Development Phase – VII

Deep overnight sprint: polishing user interfaces, telemetry dashboards, end-to-end autonomous agent execution, and benchmark testing.

03:00 AM – 04:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Review – III (Prototype & Integration Review)

Third evaluation round. Jury mentors evaluate working prototype execution, multi-agent coordination stability, and deliverable readiness.

04:00 AM – 05:00 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Development Phase – VIII

Post-review refinements: resolving edge cases identified in Review III, hardening error recovery, and final pipeline stabilization.

05:00 AM – 05:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Tea / Refreshments

Early dawn hot tea, coffee, and light refreshments as squads power into the final hours of the hackathon sprint.

05:30 AM – 07:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Final Development, Integration & Testing

Cloud deployment, container verification, automated regression tests, pitch deck preparation, and final functional checks.

07:30 AM – 08:45 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Final Review (Project Demo & Jury Evaluation)

Live interactive demonstrations and technical defenses before the grand jury panels. Scored across autonomy, novelty, architecture, and execution.

08:45 AM – 09:30 AM · DAY 2 (01.10.2026)

Breakfast

Hot South Indian breakfast buffet served at the VCET dining hall for all participants, mentors, and evaluators before the grand valedictory ceremony.

09:30 AM ONWARDS · DAY 2 (01.10.2026) · RESULTS & VALEDICTORY

Results / Valedictory Function

Grand valedictory ceremony and prize distribution. Announcement of winners, presentation of the ₹25,000 cash prizes, IEEE certificates, and closing felicitations.

GETTING TO VCET CAMPUS

Travel & Transit Guide

Convenient multimodal transit connectivity to VCET Madurai campus from all major hubs.

✈️

Madurai Airport (IXM)

11 KM · ~18 MINS

Direct flight connections from Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, New Delhi, and Colombo. Ola/Uber and pre-paid airport taxis provide hassle-free direct transit via Madurai Ring Road.

🚆

Madurai Junction (MDU)

9 KM · ~20 MINS

Major railway junction with daily express and Vande Bharat connectivity from across India. Auto rickshaws and app cabs are available 24/7 right outside the main railway concourse.

🚌

MGR Bus Stand (Mattuthavani)

8 KM · ~15 MINS

Central inter-state bus terminus with SETC and private omnibus services. Regular city buses (Lines 10, 75, Ring Road routes) stop directly at the VCET Viraganoor Ring Road gate.
